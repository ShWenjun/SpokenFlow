# 协作行为准则 / Code of conduct

讨论问题和证据，尊重不同经验和语言背景。允许建设性分歧，不接受侮辱、骚扰、歧视、威胁、冒名或泄露他人隐私。不得索取或公开他人的密码、API Key、聊天记录或个人身份信息。

Discuss problems and evidence respectfully across experience and language differences. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, impersonation and exposure of private information are not. Do not solicit or publish credentials, personal messages or identifying data.

维护者可以要求修改内容、隐藏违规评论、关闭无关讨论，或限制持续违规者参与。处置应与行为相称，并尽可能给出原因。举报时通过维护者在 GitHub 公开提供的联系渠道，只提交必要信息；不要在公开 Issue 中重复传播敏感内容。

Maintainers may request edits, hide harmful comments, close irrelevant discussions or restrict repeated abuse, proportionately and with explanations where practical. Use a contact channel explicitly provided by the maintainer; do not repost sensitive material in a public issue.

规则适用于本项目的 Issues、PR、评审和其他官方协作空间。Applies to project issues, PRs, reviews and official collaboration spaces.
