# 项目维护 / Governance

## 中文

SpokenFlow 由 ShWenjun 维护，负责代码合并、版本发布和项目文档。持续贡献者可经讨论加入维护。仓库权限根据实际职责授予。

修复通过 Pull Request 审阅。影响架构、依赖、数据发送范围或许可的改动，应先提出 Issue，说明问题、方案和验证方式。软件兼容性按实际测试记录标注。

贡献者保留各自作品的版权。许可变更须核对已有授权及相关权利人的同意。安全问题按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 处理，社区交流遵守[行为准则](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)。

## English

ShWenjun maintains SpokenFlow and is responsible for merges, releases and project documentation. Sustained contributors may join maintenance after discussion. Repository permissions follow the agreed responsibilities.

Fixes receive pull-request review. Changes to architecture, dependencies, data transmission or licensing should begin with an issue describing the problem, proposal and validation. Compatibility claims require recorded evidence.

Contributors retain copyright in their work. Licensing changes require review of existing grants and relevant rights-holder consent. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for community discussions.
